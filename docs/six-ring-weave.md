# Six Ring Weave · v0.3

**Six rings. No single lead.** Three loosely paired sets of slender upper ribs sit at staggered depths on a single illustrative chassis. Reflections supply the lower arcs; no lower physical ring exists. Viewpoint makes edges appear to meet. Nothing interlocks or moves mechanically.

From the boardwalk, look for a gathered gesture without losing the six voices. From the pier, see distinct layers cross in perspective. From the side, make the spacing feel intentional. Wakes can break the reflections without breaking the artwork: the upper forms must read on their own.

## Thread the light, not the structure

**Still → Thread → Return.** Let the eye find six edges. Pass a softly lit segment from one fixed rib to the next, then let the light settle. Try **1 → 3 → 5 → 6 → 4 → 2**, then try a different lead or go backwards. Indices and diagram colours distinguish design elements; they are not a proposed painted marking or six-colour lighting scheme. Use slow fades without glare, spill or flashes. On the real water, one rib’s reflected accent may break away while another stays legible.

The lake is the first input; real ripples are direct feedback. The preview uses simulated presets that change the path, rhythm and reflection. Actual wind and daylight sensing are not required and would need separate testing, consent where relevant, approvals and an operating envelope. If enabled later, stale inputs return to a preset; public gestures cannot reach the float, and none of these layers can alter navigation or override marine limits.

## Advance only if

1. All six ribs read with reflected geometry masked; test frontal, oblique and near-side viewpoints at multiple heights.
2. A two-rib prototype suggests a handoff from at least two viewpoints before scaling to six.
3. The cue is useful without sound, without mist and without a device in the visitor’s hands.
4. The six-rib structure earns a fresh review for wind area, torsion, centre of gravity, attachments and handling. Earlier geometry and load assumptions do not transfer.
