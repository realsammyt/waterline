Six Rings on Mother

Six fixed half rings spring from one floating ring and cross over open water. Calm water finishes them into one woven eye; a wake takes the lower half away. Face it with Shore, walk round with Weave and Edge, look down with Above, change the simulated water, add choices for the six values, and press Next hour: one thread of light passes through, then the chosen ring holds a low band of its own tone that travels from one foot to the other through the hour, with any close runners-up as a chord. A simulated hour takes 30 seconds. Audio is optional and starts off.
