# Earlier optical studies and concept art

v0.1 asked whether one low apparent arc and the lake could suggest a ring. Its optical-and-floating diagram is an original concept sketch, not a mooring or navigation plan. [SVG](../assets/diagrams/optical-floating-v01.svg) · [PNG](../assets/diagrams/optical-floating-v01.png)

v0.2 split the gesture into three fixed ribs in parallel vertical planes. The largest optical input was 6.0 × 2.0 m; the smaller ribs were staggered so the aperture could gather, open and compress as the viewer walked. There is no pupil, rotating mechanism, or guaranteed perfect ring. [Simulator](../simulators/aperture.html) · [SVG](../assets/diagrams/aperture-sightlines-v02.svg) · [PNG](../assets/diagrams/aperture-sightlines-v02.png)

v0.3 wove six slender ribs at staggered depths on one chassis, with a light handoff from rib to rib. It answered the optics but offered no familiar object, which the call's theme asks for. Its simulator became the v0.5 simulator. [Note](six-ring-weave.md) · [SVG](../assets/diagrams/six-ring-weave-v03.svg) · [PNG](../assets/diagrams/six-ring-weave-v03.png)

v0.4 set nine translucent domes on a low amber disc, the mother, and brought the Honey Foam words and the scale story. The domes were something to look at, not through, and gave no place where the work gathers into one view. [Note](honey-foam.md) · [Simulator](../simulators/foam.html) · [SVG](../assets/diagrams/foam-on-mother-v04.svg) · [PNG](../assets/diagrams/foam-on-mother-v04.png)

## Mood views, not field evidence

[Matched day/night study](../assets/concept/matched-day-night.png): synthetic AI-assisted concept art made with OpenAI ImageGen; the Toronto-inspired setting is imaginary, not a site photograph. The shared viewpoint contrasts ambient silver and restrained ember without mist. This is a mood study, not v0.5 geometry, brightness, or placement.

[Optional mist study](../assets/concept/optional-mist.png): an OpenAI ImageGen edit of the synthetic matched study illustrates a possible shallow veil in an invented setting. It does not establish field performance, operating conditions, approvals, or a commitment to mist.

The current [Six Rings on Mother](rings-on-mother.md) supersedes these forms for the next model. For real-site validation, independently survey the intended viewpoints and compare actual permitted site images to the same fixed geometry.
