# Repository instructions

- Read [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) first: it says where the work stands and what is next. Update it before you stop.
- Read README and the relevant design note. Treat v0.5 (Six Rings on Mother) as current; label older studies and dimensions as archival and provisional.
- Maintain no-network, no-build local HTML. Do not add credentials, APIs, CDNs, telemetry, hardware, sensor access, or any control link to a real artwork. The hourly public choice in v0.5 is a proposal described in the notes; in this repository it stays a local simulation.
- No private links, personal details, redistribution-uncleared assets, procurement forms, partner or commission claims, or invented legal/license language. Retain provenance for public visuals.
- Author original SVG diagrams and keep their PNG exports in sync. Label virtual reflections and simulated inputs; neither are live or structural.
- Keep navigation safety separate from decorative power and public participation. Do not elevate planning values into ratings or approved design.
- Review tests/manual.md and verify changed interactions plus failure cases. Never publish or deploy as part of routine testing.
